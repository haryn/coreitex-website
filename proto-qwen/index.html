(01) Who we are

A new center of possibility for rural East Texas.

COREI™ is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization committed to serving rural and underserved communities by bringing urban innovation to rural America.


Our vision is a one-stop multi-purpose complex that brings essential services, meaningful opportunity, and shared spaces closer to the people who need them.

Community Impact

Phase 01 — The Spark

It began with a question.

What if rural communities didn't have to choose between leaving home and going without? What if the services, opportunities, and spaces that thrive in cities could take root right here in the Piney Woods of East Texas?

2023
Phase 02 — The Vision

A blueprint for community.

The idea became a plan. Medical care, veteran services, education, sports, family support, and economic opportunity — all under one purposeful roof. A multi-purpose complex designed for generations.

2024
Phase 03 — Breaking Ground

Roots taking shape.

Partnerships formed. Land was secured. Construction began. With Crossroads Family Care and community allies, COREI™ moved from vision to visible progress on County Road 482 South in Mount Enterprise.

2025
Phase 04 — The Future

A legacy in the making.

Today, COREI™ stands as a full project in the making — a growing campus that will serve the region for decades. Every donation, every volunteer hour, every partnership brings this vision closer to reality.

2026
