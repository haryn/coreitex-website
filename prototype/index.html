WHO WE ARE

Empowering Rural Communities to Thrive

COREI™ is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization committed to serving rural and underserved communities by 'Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America™' through the development of a one-stop multi-purpose complex.

This multi-purpose Complex encompasses medical and veteran services, education and training, sports and recreation, health and fitness, childcare and after-school services, business and conference services, and community activities and events.

We are partnering with medical service providers, veteran service officers, federal and state veteran service agencies, and veteran organizations to overcome the many challenges veterans and rural residents face.

HEALTHCARE PARTNER

COREI™ is proud to collaborate with Crossroads Family Care, a cornerstone of local health services. This partnership is more than a referral; it is a shared commitment for a healthier community. Crossroads Family Care provides essential medical expertise and compassionate care that complements our support services.
