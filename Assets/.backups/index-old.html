Who We Are

COREI™ is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization committed to serving rural and underserved communities by ‘Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America™’ through the development of a one-stop multi-purpose complex.

This multi-purpose Complex will encompass medical and veteran services, education and training, sports and recreation, health and fitness, childcare and after-school services, business and conference services, and community activities and events.

We need the entire community to work with us at the multi-purpose complex to improve economic and social sustainability and encourage business diversification. We are partnering with medical service providers, veteran service officers, federal and state veteran service agencies, and veteran organizations to overcome the many challenges veterans face. We also strive to build more cohesive and stronger communities and to empower young people and families to reach their highest potential and become champions of change.

Partner Spotlight

Crossroads Family Care

COREI™ is proud to collaborate with Crossroads Family Care, a cornerstone of local health services. This partnership is more than a referral; it is a shared commitment for a healthier community. Crossroads Family Care provides essential medical expertise and compassionate care that complements our support services. Together, we are creating a stronger, more resilient network for all who need us. Discover their full range of medical services on their official website.
