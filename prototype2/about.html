About COREI™ · Non-Profit Organization

Bringing Urban Innovation
To Rural America™

Community One Resources Initiatives Corporation (COREI™) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to empowering rural and underserved communities in East Texas through a revolutionary one-stop multi-purpose complex.

Our Vision

Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America™

COREI™ believes that innovation is not a one-time engagement, but an ongoing commitment to refine resources, facilities, and services. Our vision is to elevate rural quality of life with world-class infrastructure.

Our Mission

Empowerment & Sustainability

To develop a better quality of life for people within underserved communities through initiatives that improve economic and social sustainability through charity, education and training, sports and recreation, medical and health services, fitness, and advocacy.

Strategic Pillars

Our Mission Focus

01 · Action

Empowerment & Action

To unite residents and provide resources, building a stronger community where empowered individuals create lasting change and shared prosperity together.

02 · Belonging

Connection & Belonging

To cultivate connection and mutual support, weaving together the fabric of the community so that every voice is heard, and every person feels at home.

03 · Vision

Vision & Future

We are focused on a future built by us, for us—uplifting local voices and driving the initiatives that will define our community's long-term success.

Project Scope

14 Core Strategic Goals

The multi-purpose complex encompasses comprehensive facilities to support economic sustainability, health, education, and community unity:

1
Create skilled jobs.
2
Establish businesses through leased space.
3
Enhance broadband communication.
4
Create a state-of-the-art sports, education, fitness, and conference complex.
5
Develop sports programs and initiatives.
6
Offer vocational and certification programs and virtual educational classes.
7
Provide computer labs.
8
Build affordable housing for veterans and seniors.
9
Provide a medical, wellness, and veteran services center.
10
Offer childcare, Pre-K, and Kid Zone services.
11
Develop an outdoor kids' playground, park, and event area (weddings, family reunions, concerts, etc.).
12
Provide a food service café.
13
Build living quarters (hotel, Airbnb, motel, or garden-type units).
14
Host church meetings, retreats, and conventions.
