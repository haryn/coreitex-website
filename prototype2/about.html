About COREI™ · Non-Profit Organization

Bringing Urban Innovation
To Rural America™

Community One Resources Initiatives Corporation (COREI™) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to empowering rural and underserved communities in East Texas through state-of-the-art facilities, economic opportunities, and vital human services.

Our Vision

Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America™

COREI™ believes that innovation is not a one-time engagement, but an ongoing process to refine products, operations, and services. Our vision is to build a vibrant epicenter where health, opportunity, recreation, education, and community grow hand-in-hand right here in East Texas.

COREI Multi-Purpose Complex Mount Enterprise, Texas · Master Site Development

Our Mission

Empowerment & Sustainability

To develop a better quality of life for people within underserved communities through initiatives that improve economic and social sustainability. COREI™ will accomplish this through charity, education and training, sports and recreation, medical and health services, fitness, and proactive advocacy.

To unite residents and provide resources, building a stronger community where empowered individuals create lasting change and shared prosperity together.

To cultivate connection and mutual support, weaving together the fabric of the community so that every voice is heard, and every person feels at home.

“We are focused on a future built by us, for us—uplifting local voices and driving the initiatives that will define our community’s success.”

Organizational Foundation

Our Core Values

COREI™ implements a proactive approach that reinforces these four core values and embraces change and flexibility to foster community resilience and long-term growth.

Community

Cultivating strong relationships with the community, encouraging residents to embrace change, learn together, and respond effectively.

Empowerment

Providing members with the necessary tools, resources, and environment to thrive, leading to increased motivation and purpose.

Integrity

Operating with complete transparency, ethical governance, and steadfast commitment to 501(c)(3) public service principles.

Innovation

Bringing urban-level technology, infrastructure, and forward-thinking facilities to rural East Texas communities.
