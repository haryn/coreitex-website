About COREI™ · Non-Profit Organization

Bringing Urban Innovation
To Rural America™

Community One Resources Initiatives Corporation (COREI™) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization committed to serving rural and underserved communities by building a state-of-the-art multi-purpose complex in East Texas.

Our Vision

Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America™

COREI™ believes that innovation is not a one-time engagement, but an ongoing process to refine products, operations, and services. Our vision is to build a place where health, opportunity, recreation, education, and community can grow together right here in East Texas.

Our Mission

Empowerment & Sustainability

To develop a better quality of life for people within underserved communities through initiatives that improve economic and social sustainability. COREI™ will do this through charity, education and training, sports and recreation, medical and health services, fitness, and advocacy.

Organizational Foundation

Our Core Values

COREI™ implements a proactive approach that reinforces these four core values and embraces change and flexibility to foster community resilience and growth.

Community

Cultivating strong relationships with the community, encouraging residents to embrace change, learn together, and respond effectively.

Empowerment

Providing members with the necessary tools, resources, and environment to thrive, leading to increased motivation and purpose.

Integrity

Operating with complete transparency, ethical governance, and steadfast commitment to 501(c)(3) public service principles.

Innovation

Bringing urban-level technology, infrastructure, and forward-thinking facilities to rural East Texas communities.

Board & Leadership Commitments

Leadership & Purpose

Commitment to Empowerment

Empowering Every Resident

COREI™ leadership is committed to providing community members with the necessary tools, resources, and environment to thrive, leading to increased motivation, productivity, and a strong sense of value and purpose across generations.

Commitment to Innovation

Relentless Innovation

COREI™ was founded on the principle of bringing urban innovation to rural America. COREI™ believes that innovation is not a one-time engagement, but an ongoing process to refine facilities, operations, and support services. This commitment is our passion.
