What We Do
Empowering East Texas

COREI™ is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization committed to serving rural and underserved communities by "Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America™" through the development of a one-stop multi-purpose complex.

This multi-purpose complex will encompass medical and veteran services, education and training, sports and recreation, health and fitness, childcare and after-school services, business and conference services, and community activities and events. We need the entire community to work with us at the multi-purpose complex to improve economic and social sustainability and encourage business diversification.

Unlocking Potential

Education & Youth Empowerment

At COREI™, our educational and leadership partners design enrichment initiatives that inspire young people to realize their full potential. These initiatives focus on leadership, motivation, self-esteem, skill-building and confidence, the importance of education, and entrepreneurship to help them construct the best path for their lives and future. We want to ensure that every child is given the same opportunity to achieve and excel.

A Safe Place For Every Child To Grow

Childcare & After-School Program

Finding quality childcare and Pre-K should not be a struggle. We partner with local educators to provide after-school programs that go beyond basic supervision. We take a heart-centered approach, focusing on each child's unique potential. From developing social skills to fostering emotional well-being, our goal is to create a nurturing environment where children feel safe to be themselves, engage with others, and discover the joy of friendship.

Active Living For All Ages

Sports & Recreation

At COREI™, we believe that staying active is the key to a happy and healthy life. COREI™ is committed to fostering active lifestyles for both youth and seniors through comprehensive sports and recreation initiatives. Our sports and recreation programs are designed to bring the community together, helping people of all ages build lasting friendships and strong self-esteem through friendly competition. Whether it is athletic competition, learning to swim safely, or finding a new favorite hobby, we provide a supportive environment where everyone—from children to older adults—can find joy in social, emotional, and physical development.

Building Stronger Communities Through Wellness

Health & Fitness

COREI™ is dedicated to the well-being of every community we serve. Through strategic partnerships with fitness experts, we provide accessible health programs designed specifically for rural and underserved communities. Whether you prefer a guided plan or a self-designed workout, our goal is to help you achieve total wellness for your body, mind, and soul. We know that when a community stays active together, it builds more than just fitness—it creates stronger social bonds and a more prosperous future for everyone.

Empowering Communities & Creating Change

Community & Social Responsibility

COREI™ collaborates with strategic partners to design initiatives that strengthen rural and underserved areas. We provide youth and local residents with the essential tools and resources needed to engage, learn, and thrive. By equipping the next generation with confidence and insight, we empower them to tackle local challenges head-on and become true champions for change within their communities.

Dedicated Healthcare Access

Medical Services

At COREI™, we believe that true healthcare is built on a foundation of trust and local collaboration. Our approach combines clinical services with essential social supports, focusing on preventive care and innovative technology to reach underserved rural areas. Through strategic partnerships with providers like Crossroads Family Care, we connect community members directly to essential clinical expertise, preventive checkups, and health education.

Serving Those Who Served

Veterans Services

COREI™ is deeply committed to our veterans, helping them overcome the hurdles of transitioning to civilian life, navigating VA benefits, managing physical health struggles, and securing education and employment. By tackling complex issues such as housing stability, mental health support, and direct service access alongside government and veteran service partners, we help veterans reclaim their future with confidence, honor, and community backing.
