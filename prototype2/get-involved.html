Advocacy

Become an Advocate for Change

We need advocates for change to assist COREI™ in "Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America." We need individuals and/or groups to actively work with COREI™ to influence and make impactful transformations in Rural America.

✓
To work and engage with rural community leaders, local representatives, funders and state decision-makers to support and bring to fruition the vision and mission of COREI™.
✓
To educate and inspire the people of rural communities via COREI™ initiatives, tools and resources to empower, motivate and build self-confidence to strengthen and increase a sense of value and purpose within one's self.
✓
To foster collaboration between COREI™ and rural community leaders, groups and individuals around issues and concerns that impact rural communities.
✓
To act as a bridge between COREI™ and rural communities, to guide and build support for the innovative initiatives of COREI™ that would bring impactful economic opportunities within the rural communities.

Participation Roles

Ways to Participate

Membership

Become a Member

We are looking for members who would like to join us in carrying out the COREI™ vision and mission in "Bringing Urban Innovation to Rural America." The member candidates must be compassionate and willing to serve and help rural communities to become more productive in achieving economic and social sustainability.

Volunteer

Get Involved as a Volunteer

We are looking for volunteers to join us to help us serve and empower rural communities to meet the needs that they are lacking. The volunteer candidates must be willing to serve and unite people within the rural communities and strengthen their sense of value and purpose. Become a Volunteer Today!

Non-Profit Workforce

Become a Worker

COREI™ is looking for nonprofit workers to perform tasks like content creation, fundraising support, data analysis, document searching, donor communications, volunteer scheduling, accounting, financial reporting, and budgeting to ensure operational excellence.

Strategic Collaboration

Partner with COREI™

We are looking for partners to help COREI™ expand its reach, increase fundraising, and enhance credibility. Partnering with reputable organizations amplifies public trust and accelerates rural development.

1. Expanded Network

Access a partner's network, customers, and audience to broaden the reach of our vision and mission across Texas and beyond.

2. Increased Revenue

Establish partnerships that enable sustainable funding generation through sponsorships, joint events, and donor contributions.

3. Enhanced Credibility

Partnering with reputable organizations expands public trust and reinforces confidence in the mission of COREI™.

4. Access to Resources

Gain access to a partner's resources such as facility space, professional expertise, or technology to amplify community impact.

5. Improved Efficiency

Share administrative costs, expertise, and resources to enable COREI™ to run highly effective and cost-efficient programs.
