Your generous support makes a profound impact on East Texas. By contributing to COREI™, you help us build a one-stop multi-purpose complex that delivers essential medical care, veteran support, youth education, fitness facilities, and economic opportunities to rural communities.

501(c)(3) Public Charity

Tax-Deductible Charitable Contributions

Community One Resources Initiatives Corporation (COREI™) is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Every contribution—whether a one-time gift, a monthly pledge, a complex sponsorship, or an equipment grant—directly funds local transformation and is tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.

Required Legal Tax Disclosure Statement:
Community One Resources Initiatives Corporation (COREI™) is recognized by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity. Contributions are deductible under Section 170 of the Internal Revenue Code. Donors will receive an official tax receipt for all qualifying donations. To verify tax-exempt status or view annual non-profit tax filings, visit the official IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search (TEOS).

Ways You Can Give to COREI™

01 · Direct Giving

Direct & Monthly Giving

Make a one-time gift or establish a recurring monthly donation to sustain core non-profit operations, youth enrichment, and community programs. Monthly giving provides reliable funding that allows COREI™ to plan long-term community projects with confidence.

02 · Capital Campaign

Facility & Complex Naming Rights

Leave a lasting legacy by sponsoring key wings of the upcoming multi-purpose complex, including the Medical Wing, Veteran Center, Education Hub, Sports Complex, Family Center, or Conference Hall. Naming opportunities immortalize your dedication to East Texas.

03 · In-Kind Contributions

Equipment & Resource Grants

Donate essential technology, medical checkup equipment, athletic supplies, or educational software to directly outfit our classrooms, computer labs, and wellness facilities for youth and seniors.

04 · Partnerships

Corporate Partnerships & Event Sponsorships

Partner your business or foundation with COREI™ to fund specific initiatives, youth sports tournaments, vocational training workshops, or community events, driving shared prosperity and public goodwill.
