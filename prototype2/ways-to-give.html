Ways You Can Give to COREI™

01 · Direct Giving

Direct & Monthly Giving

Make a one-time gift or establish a recurring monthly donation to sustain core non-profit operations, youth enrichment, and community programs. Monthly giving provides reliable funding that allows COREI™ to plan long-term community projects with confidence.

02 · Capital Campaign

Facility & Complex Naming Rights

Leave a lasting legacy by sponsoring key wings of the upcoming multi-purpose complex, including the Medical Wing, Veteran Center, Education Hub, Sports Complex, Family Center, or Conference Hall. Naming opportunities immortalize your dedication to East Texas.

Donate essential technology, medical checkup equipment, athletic supplies, or educational software to directly outfit our classrooms, computer labs, and wellness facilities for youth and seniors.

Corporate Partnerships & Event Sponsorships

Partner your business or foundation with COREI™ to fund specific initiatives, youth sports tournaments, vocational training workshops, or community events, driving shared prosperity and public goodwill.
